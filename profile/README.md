# SpatioRisk Intelligence

## Spatial Intelligence. De-Risk Insights. Resilient Decisions.
SpatioRisk Intelligence is a geospatial intelligence and decision-support consultancy focused on helping organizations discover opportunities, quantify uncertainty, and build resilience through advanced analytics, machine learning, Earth systems science, and spatial risk assessment.
We integrate geoscience, geospatial data, artificial intelligence, statistical modelling, and climate intelligence to support smarter decisions across natural resource, environmental, infrastructure, and public-sector challenges.

---
## Our Mission
To empower organizations with science-driven insights that improve exploration success, resource development, environmental stewardship, and climate resilience.

## Current Projects

### Bathurst VMS Prospectivity AI
Machine learning-based mineral prospectivity mapping for the Bathurst Mining Camp, New Brunswick.
Key components:
- Airborne geophysics integration
- Geological and geochemical data fusion
- Random Forest and XGBoost modelling
- Explainable AI (SHAP)
- Spatial validation workflows
- Exploration targeting
**Status:** Manuscript prepared for peer-reviewed journal submission.

---
### NB-Geo-AI
Development of deep-learning workflows for geological contact mapping using airborne geophysical datasets.
**Status:** Active development.

---
### Aqua-Predict-NB
Groundwater forecasting and water-risk analysis using hydrogeological, geological, and climate datasets.
**Status:** Proof-of-concept development.
